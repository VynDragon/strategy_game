# Main Building Elements

## Levels of detail

- Global:

System map, no buildings visible, planets and elements hosting strategic level grids are visible.
Stations are also visible at this level despite being local grids.
No grid.
Only orbits are accessible as areas where things can be created (for stations)
This is the level where ships are visible.
Scale is ?

- Strategic:

Planet or element map (asteroids ?). Contains elements such as bases, depots, or Local grids elements.
This is the level where groups of units are visible.
Cities occupate an area at the strategic level (so they dont overlap).
Scale is 512 units = 25 meters (standard / 25)

- Local:

Both Real time battlefields, and actual environments.
Battlefields only exist for a fight, unless the point of battle is a city or a station with a local grid.
Scale is 512 units = 1 meters (standard)
